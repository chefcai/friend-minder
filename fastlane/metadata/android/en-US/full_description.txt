Friend-minder helps you stay in touch with the people who matter, without the mental overhead of remembering who you haven't called in a while.

FEATURES

Contact reminders
Get gentle nudges to reach out to friends and family based on how long it's been since your last contact - with a frequency you can customize per person, per group, or as a global default.

Groups
Organize contacts into color-coded groups (family, close friends, work, etc.) so you can see at a glance who belongs where, and manage group membership from either the group or the contact.

Overall History
See your current outreach streak, a monthly outreach chart, and your full outreach history across every contact, all in one place.

Outreach logging
Send the suggested message straight from a reminder and it is logged automatically - date, time, and type recorded with no extra step. Your stats and streaks update immediately.

Contact detail
Every contact shows their outreach history, special dates (birthdays and custom dates you add), group memberships, and a per-contact check-in frequency you can override anytime.

Birthdays & special dates
Friend-minder can pull birthdays from your phone's contacts and combine them with custom dates you add (anniversaries, and anything else worth remembering), with optional reminder notifications.

Privacy-first
No accounts, no cloud sync, no ads, no trackers. Everything lives on your device, backed up via Android's own Auto Backup if you have that enabled. Friend-minder is free and open source, with no accounts and no trackers.
